The paper connects large language model simulation with social and cognitive science to investigate the gap between what individuals privately believe and what they are willing to express publicly.

- Hajime Hotta and Minh-Tien Nguyen, "From Private Beliefs to Public Silence: A Multi-agent LLM Simulation of Psychological Safety," in *Proceedings of the Annual Meeting of the Cognitive Science Society*, vol. 48, 2026.
