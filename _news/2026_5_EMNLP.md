The two papers study whether language models explain emotions for the right structural reasons and how psychologically grounded cognitive projections can improve retrieval-augmented conversational mental health support.

- Hajime Hotta and Minh-Tien Nguyen, "Right for the Right Reasons? Evaluating the Structural Fidelity of LLM Emotion Explanations," in *Proceedings of the 2026 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP)*, 2026 (accepted).

- Minh-Tien Nguyen, Huu-Loi Le, Manh-Cuong Phan, Duong-Hieu Le, Dinh-Phuc Pham, Van-Quyet Nguyen, and Hajime Hotta, "Reframe to Retrieve: Cognition-aware RAG with Projections Inspired by Appraisal and CBT for Conversational Mental Health Support," in *Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2026* (accepted).
