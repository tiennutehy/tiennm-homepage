- Bao-Duc Vu, Ngoc Anh Le, Huu Duc Anh Nguyen, Kiem-Hieu Nguyen, Minh-Tien Nguyen, and Hajime Hotta, "Two Birds with One Stone: From Social Posts to BigFive via Personality-aware Multi-trait Inference," *Affective Computing and Intelligent Interaction (ACII)*, 2026 (accepted).
